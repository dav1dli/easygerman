## Book-specific notes
- Every story is one unit with a `## <German title>` heading (no "Kapitel N"). Translate the titles; where a title is a pun or allusion, find a German title that works the same way (e.g. «Das Geschenk der Weisen» for The Gift of the Magi).
- O. Henry's voice depends on mock-erudite narration, slang, dialect (Southern, Western, New York street talk), puns and twist endings. Keep the narrator's ironic asides. Render dialect with a light German colloquial colouring (not a specific German dialect), and keep it consistent for each character.
- Keep American setting and realia: dollars and cents, Broadway, the Bowery, ranches, rangers. Gloss them briefly in the text when needed.
- First-person narrators who tell their stories in slang (Jeff Peters, Andy Tucker and others) speak casually: Perfekt and colloquial word order are allowed in their narration at every stage, but the stage's vocabulary and grammar ceiling still applies.
