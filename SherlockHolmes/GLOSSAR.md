# Glossar: Sherlock Holmes

Keep English names in their original form (Sherlock Holmes, Dr. John H. Watson, Mrs. Hudson, Lestrade, Gregson, Mycroft Holmes, Professor Moriarty, Irene Adler, Sir Henry Baskerville, Dr. Mortimer, Stapleton). Holmes and Watson say «Sie» to each other.
